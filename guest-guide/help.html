Frequently asked questions

Where is breakfast served?

Breakfast is served on the 1st floor. Weekdays: 7:30–9:30 AM. Weekends: 8:00–10:00 AM.

Can I add breakfast after booking?

Yes. Please speak to Reception to ask for breakfast to be added to your stay.

How do I request housekeeping?

Stayover housekeeping is available on request. Tell Reception and include your room number.

Where can I get extra towels or toiletries?

Ask Reception for extra towels, toilet rolls, toiletries, tea and coffee supplies or other room essentials.

What should I do if my key card stops working?

Bring the card to Reception for assistance. Keep it away from phones and magnetic items.

Does the hotel have parking?

The hotel does not provide on-site parking. Use the Travel & Parking page to compare nearby options and official Cardiff Council guidance.

How do I report a problem?

Please contact Reception as soon as possible and give your room number, what happened and when you first noticed it.
